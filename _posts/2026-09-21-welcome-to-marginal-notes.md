I'm starting this blog as a place to think through economic data and
ideas in public, partly for myself, partly for anyone else who finds it
useful.

A few things you can expect here:

- Short write-ups on labor market data as it comes out (JOLTS, the
  employment situation, wage growth) and what it actually means.
- Occasional deeper dives into a dataset or a question I can't stop
  thinking about.
- No hot takes for the sake of hot takes. If I don't have something
  worth saying about a release, I'll skip it.

More soon. In the meantime, feel free to poke around or reach out.
